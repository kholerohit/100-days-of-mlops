# Hint: Capstone (3 of 4) - GitOps Continuous Deployment with ArgoCD

## Topic and approach

GitOps deployment should be driven by a committed desired state, with ArgoCD reconciling that revision. Trace the image or manifest change from commit to sync status and confirm rollback is another auditable Git change.
