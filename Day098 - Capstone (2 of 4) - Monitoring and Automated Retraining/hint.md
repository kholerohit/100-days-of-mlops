# Hint: Capstone (2 of 4) - Monitoring and Automated Retraining

## Topic and approach

Monitoring-driven retraining needs an observable threshold and a workflow trigger with clear success/failure states. Check that the metric is populated, the threshold is actionable, and retraining does not promote a model without evaluation.

## Work independently

Use this as a starting point, then confirm the task requirements and validate the resulting state before consulting the solution.
